# Role Salaries (GEL)

Reference table of **GROSS** vs **NET** monthly salaries per role.
The **~21.6% employee tax** (pension + income tax) is the difference between
gross and net.

## Full Time — Support Associate (SA)

| Seniority    | Gross (GEL) | Net (GEL) |
| ------------ | ----------: | --------: |
| 0 – 6 months |       1,704 |  1,335.93 |
| 6+ months    |       1,830 |  1,434.72 |

## Part Time — Support Associate (PT SA)

Part-time salaries are **half** of the full-time SA values.

| Seniority    | Gross (GEL) | Net (GEL) |
| ------------ | ----------: | --------: |
| 0 – 6 months |         852 |    667.96 |
| 6+ months    |         915 |    717.36 |

---

### Notes

- The calculator uses **NET** salaries only for its main calculations
  (deductions, additions, insurance, Fitpass, snacks, unpaid leaves, etc.).
- **GROSS** values are shown here purely as a reference and are also used to
  render the _"before tax"_ line in the results card.
- **Tax rate**: `(gross − net) / gross ≈ 21.6 %` across every role.
- Only **SA** and **PT SA** have two tiers (0–6 months vs 6+ months).
  **SL** and **SSL** each have a single flat salary regardless of tenure.
